Can we speak before our drawings are ready?

Yes. Share your site location, approximate area and the type of home you have in mind. The team can discuss what information is needed for a useful next conversation.

How is a construction estimate prepared?

An estimate depends on drawings, site conditions, built-up area, specifications and scope. A generic square-foot rate is not a substitute for a project-specific scope and estimate.

Which locations can I enquire about?

Leaf Infra’s website identifies Chennai and Tamil Nadu as its service area. Share your exact site location so the team can confirm availability.

Can I visit a completed project?

Ask the team about available references and possible site visits. Access depends on the project owner’s permission and site conditions.
