Project enquiry privacy notice

Last updated: 28 October 2026

When you submit an enquiry, Leaf Infra collects your name, phone number, optional email, project details, optional land status, budget range, intended timeline, visual preferences and campaign source parameters. We use this information to respond, understand your requirements and follow up about your project.

How your enquiry is handled

Enquiries are stored in the website’s Cloudflare database. Where configured, notifications are sent using Resend email and Meta WhatsApp. These services process enquiry information to deliver it to the team. We do not sell enquiry details.

Security checks

Cloudflare Turnstile helps protect the form from automated abuse. A salted hash of your IP address is used temporarily to limit repeated submissions; raw IP addresses are not stored in our enquiry database. Hosting and security providers may process technical connection data.

Your choices

You can call us instead of using the form. To ask about your enquiry data, request correction or deletion, or stop follow-up, email infraleaf@gmail.com or call +91 93455 23646.

Retention and analytics

We keep enquiry details while responding and managing the project relationship, and remove them when no longer needed, subject to applicable record-keeping requirements. This build does not load advertising or analytics trackers. Click and successful submission events are available for a future analytics integration.
