About Leaf Infra

Construction experience.
A personal commitment.

Formerly SS Constructions, Leaf Infra works across residential, commercial and institutional construction in Chennai and Tamil Nadu.

Our story

Built on experience.
Focused on what’s next.

Leaf Infra’s original website traces the company’s journey to 2010. Its published experience includes school campuses and residential development projects, supported by an in-house construction team.

Today, the residential focus is on villas, independent homes and larger family residences. The starting point remains practical: understand the project, define the scope and coordinate the execution.
